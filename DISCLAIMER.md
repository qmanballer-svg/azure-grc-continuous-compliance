# Disclaimer

This repository is an educational cybersecurity GRC portfolio simulation.

All organizations, tenants, identities, cloud resources, settings, evidence, risks, findings, and remediation items are fictional. No real customer, employer, government, Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), confidential information, production credentials, secrets, or proprietary data are included.

The automated checks are demonstrations of continuous-compliance concepts. They are not an official CMMC assessment, certification, audit, attestation, legal opinion, or substitute for qualified professional judgment. A single automated signal cannot establish whether a CMMC practice is MET because assessment objectives may require additional examine, interview, and test evidence.
